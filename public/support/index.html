Voicely Support

Voicely records meetings and turns them into text on your device. Questions, bugs or suggestions: email hellotaotao@gmail.com.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad with iOS / iPadOS 18 or later.
  • Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later. Intel Macs are not supported.
  • About 1.4 GB of free space on iPhone and iPad, 1.5 GB on Mac. Most of it is the speech models, which come with the app so nothing is downloaded later.

Getting Started

  1. Open Voicely and allow microphone access when asked.
  2. Tap the record button. Text appears while you record.
  3. Tap stop. The transcript is finished a few moments later.
  4. Tap a sentence to play the audio from there. Use search and folders to find notes later.

You can also import an existing audio file; it is transcribed the same way.

Languages

The default is Auto Detect, which handles many languages and recordings that mix them, for example Chinese with English. Long English passages are transcribed with a dedicated English model.

Choose English next to the record button (or as the default in Settings) when a recording is English only: it is faster and uses less memory. If you picked English by mistake, tap Switch to Auto Detect while recording and the whole recording is treated as Auto Detect.

Locking the Screen

Recording and live transcription keep running when you lock the screen; the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show the recording. On iOS 26 and later, transcription can also finish after you stop and lock the device. If a transcript is still unfinished, open Voicely and it continues from where it stopped.

To avoid a long unwanted tail when you forget to stop, set a recording length or stop time with the timer button before or during a recording. Voicely reminds you a few minutes before it stops, and you can extend it.

iCloud Sync

With iCloud on for Voicely, notes and transcripts appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple account. Audio downloads when you play it. Sync timing is up to iCloud and can take a while after a long time offline.

To keep notes on one device only, turn off iCloud for Voicely in your device's Settings.

Troubleshooting

Recording does not start

Check that microphone access for Voicely is on in Settings (on Mac: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone), and that no other app, such as a call, is holding the microphone.

The recording is silent

Look at the waveform while recording. If it stays flat, the wrong input is selected: check the microphone or headset in use, and on Mac the input device in System Settings > Sound.

Poor transcript quality

Place the device close to the speakers and away from noise. For English-only recordings, choose English. To try again, open the note and tap Re-transcribe; the current text is replaced only when the new one is complete.

Notes missing on another device

Confirm both devices use the same Apple account, iCloud is on for Voicely, and there is enough iCloud storage. Open Voicely on both devices and give sync a few minutes.
