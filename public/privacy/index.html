Voicely Privacy Policy

Voicely records meetings and transcribes them on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. The developer does not collect any of your data.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No developer server. Voicely never sends your audio or text to the developer or to any third party.
  • Speech recognition runs on your device. The speech models are included in the app, so transcription needs no internet connection.

Recordings and Transcripts

Audio you record or import into Voicely, the transcripts it creates, and the titles and folders you give your notes are stored on your device. They are used only to show, play, search and transcribe your notes in the app.

iCloud Sync

If iCloud is turned on for Voicely, your notes sync between your devices through your own iCloud account: notes and transcripts in your private iCloud database, audio in your iCloud Drive. This data is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot access it.

To keep everything on one device, turn off iCloud for Voicely in your device's Settings. Voicely works the same without it.

Permissions

Microphone. Used only to record when you start a recording. Without it you can still import audio files and read existing notes.

Notifications. Used only when you set a recording to stop at a certain time: Voicely reminds you shortly before it stops and confirms when the recording is saved. These are local notifications created on your device.

Sharing

Nothing leaves Voicely unless you send it yourself, for example by copying a transcript or using the share button. Where it goes then is up to you and the app you share to.

Deleting Your Data

Deleting a note deletes its audio and transcript. With iCloud on, the deletion syncs to your other devices. Deleting the app removes its data from that device; data in your iCloud account can be removed in your device's iCloud storage settings.

Children

Voicely is not directed to children. Since it collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
